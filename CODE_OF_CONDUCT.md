# Code of Conduct

We want a professional, constructive and inclusive community. Be respectful, focus criticism on ideas and code, welcome good-faith questions, and protect other people's private information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sharing credentials or customer data, and deliberate security exploitation are not acceptable. Report concerns privately through the security contact described in [SECURITY.md](docs/SECURITY.md). Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect participants and the project.
